# Send-gate checklist

Run this on every campaign, right before you hit send. Five points. Every one has to be a
real yes, not a "close enough."

- [ ] **The signal is verified and dated.** You personally opened the source link for
      every company still on the list, and the date is inside your freshness window (see
      `signal-picker.md`). Not the tool's word for it, you checked it yourself.
- [ ] **One signal, one campaign.** This send uses exactly one signal type, on its own
      list. Nothing here is loaded in as a "variant" alongside a different signal. See
      Step 4 of `five-step-system.md` for why that breaks the whole point.
- [ ] **The message names the signal in line one.** The first line of the email or DM
      says what actually happened at their company, specifically, not a generic opener
      that could apply to anyone on the list.
- [ ] **Line two is the end result, not the pain.** Line two says what the signal means
      for them, in plain words. It is not a guess about their problems, and it is not
      your offer yet.
- [ ] **The first touch is under 64 words.** Count it. A first message that reads like a
      pitch, not a note from someone who noticed something real, will get the same
      silence as the name-swap version you're trying to fix.

If any box is a no, stop and fix it before you send. A campaign that fails this list will
read exactly like the mail-merge email it was built to replace.
