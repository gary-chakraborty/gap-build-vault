# Signal picker

Six buying-signal categories that work across most B2B markets. Pick one, run it through
Step 1 of `five-step-system.md`, and write your own one-sentence signal definition before
you build a list against it.

| Signal | Where to find it, free | How fresh it needs to be | What it implies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiring for a specific role | The company's careers page, plus Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby job boards, plus Indeed | Last 90 days | They're building capacity in that exact function right now, and the process behind it is still being figured out. The best window to show up with a better way to do it. |
| New funding round | Crunchbase, funding news sites, the company's own press page | Last 90 days | New money comes with new reporting and scaling obligations the old setup wasn't built to carry |
| New executive in a relevant seat | The company's news or "leadership" page, LinkedIn "started a new position" posts, press releases | Last 12 months | A new leader re-examines who's doing what and why. This is when a genuinely new budget conversation opens, not a locked-in one. |
| Tool or vendor change | Job posts that name the new tool, case studies the vendor publishes, G2 and Capterra reviews mentioning a switch | Last 90 days | Swapping a core tool means workflows are already being rebuilt. The door most people wait for is already open. |
| New location or market expansion | Local business journals, the company's own news page, "now open" or "now serving" posts | Last 90 days | Growth outpaced the systems built for the smaller version of the business, and something is about to break under the new size |
| A spike in reviews, good or bad | G2, Capterra, Google Business reviews, Trustpilot | Last 30 days | Something changed operationally recently enough that people are reacting to it in public, worth finding out what before you reach out |

Two rules that apply to every row above:
1. **The signal has to be checkable by a stranger.** If you can't paste a link to the page
   where you found it, it isn't a signal yet. It's a hunch.
2. **One company can have more than one signal.** If it does, pick the one you can verify
   fastest and build the message around that one. Don't stack two signals into one line.
   It reads as a list of facts, not a reason to reach out now.
