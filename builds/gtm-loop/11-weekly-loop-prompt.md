# 11 · The weekly loop (step 10: what worked goes back to step 1)

Every Monday. Fifteen minutes. Paste last week's numbers in.

**How to count, before you paste anything:**
- Bottom number = **new people reached** (first messages only). Never emails sent; that counts
  every follow-up again and makes every rate look about half its size.
- Booked = from your calendar's own records, first-time bookers only. Not from a typed tracker.
- Split everything by the reason you wrote to each person (hiring, funding, new leader, a public
  post...) and by channel. If you only have a blended number, say so.
- If a tool would not load, write UNKNOWN. Never write 0 for a number you could not read.

```text
Here are last week's numbers. My business file is in project knowledge.

Week: [DATES]
By channel and by reason for writing:
[reason / channel / new people reached / replies / positive replies / calls booked / calls held]
Calls held: [how many] · Decisions asked for: [how many] · Signed: [how many]
Call scores this week (from step 9): [paste the "where it slipped" lines]
Anything that broke: [paste]

Do this:
1. Find the first step where people drop off the most compared with the step before it.
   Name it in one line.
2. Tell me if the sample is too small to trust. Under 10 positive replies on a line, say
   "too small to call" and do not call a winner.
3. From the replies and the call scores, pull the exact words buyers used this week that are
   not already in my business file.
4. Give me ONE change for next week, in exactly one of these places:
   step 1 (who we research), step 3 (the offer line), step 4 (which reason we write about).
   Say what to change, the new wording, and what number would tell us it worked.
5. Write the 3 lines I should add to my business file, so next week's agents start from it.
```

**You know this worked when:** next week's list, offer line or reason for writing is different
because of something this week showed, and your business file says why.
