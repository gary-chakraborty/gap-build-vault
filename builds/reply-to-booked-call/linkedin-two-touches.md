# LinkedIn: two touches, then stop

The LinkedIn half of the system. Same rule as email: two touches to a cold person, never more.

## Pick the lane before you build

LinkedIn has two ways in, and they do not reach the same people.

| Lane | Who it reaches | What happens to everyone else |
|---|---|---|
| **Connect** (connection request with a note, then one message after they accept) | Anyone at 2nd or 3rd degree | Nothing. It works for everyone |
| **Free message** (InMail to an Open Profile) | Only people who switched on Open Profile | LinkedIn cancels the send without telling you |

So: send the free message only to people you have checked are Open Profile. Everyone else goes on the Connect lane.
Build the two as **separate sequences with separate lists**. One mixed list quietly throws away every message
LinkedIn will not deliver.

## The two touches

| Touch | Connect lane | Free message lane |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Connection request. The note names the thing that changed at their company. Under 300 characters | One message. Line one is the thing that changed. Under 64 words |
| 2 | After they accept: one message, one question they can answer with a fact | One bump, 3 to 4 days later. One question |
| Then | Stop | Stop |

**Why one question that needs a fact.** "Is this something you're thinking about?" gets "yes" or silence. "How many new
clients came in last month without a referral?" gets a number, and a number is a conversation.

## Things that break

- **The sequence freezes when it starts.** In most LinkedIn tools you can edit the text of a live sequence but you
  cannot add a step. Decide the touch count before you press start.
- **A friendly "thanks for connecting" is not a touch.** It uses up the moment. Touch 2 is the question.
- **Warm people are not cold people.** Someone who viewed your profile, commented on your post, or messaged you first
  gets the [warm reply ladder](warm-reply-ladder.md), not this.
