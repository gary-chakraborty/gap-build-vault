# The booking desk

What happens between "sounds good" and the call actually taking place. This is where booked calls quietly die: no
reminder, no plan for a no-show, nobody calling the person who went quiet.

## One person owns it

One setter owns every booked call, start to finish. Not "the team". A name. When two people share it, each one
assumes the other sent the reminder.

## The call list: two columns, never more

The setter dials from one list per stage (today's calls, no-shows, gone quiet). Each row has the name, the number, and
exactly two columns of context:

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **What happened** | 2 to 4 plain sentences: how they found you, what they said, where it stands. Ends with the first line to say on the phone |
| **Their last message** | Their exact words, then one line on what it was replying to |

Never split the story into five columns (what they said, what we said, sentiment, tags). A setter on a live call
cannot put five cells back together. If something is missing, write it down ("no phone number found"), never leave
a blank.

## After the booking

| When | What goes out | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Right after they book | A short confirmation in the same thread they replied in | Same as they came in |
| The day before | One line: the time, and one question about their business for the call | Same thread |
| 1 hour before | Only if they have not confirmed | Text or LinkedIn |
| No-show, same day | "Missed you today, happens to everyone. Want to pick a new time?" Two times offered | Same thread |
| No-show, 2 days later | One more try, then stop | Phone call |

Run the no-show and the gone-quiet messages as a **follow-up sequence the person is moved into**, never as a new
cold sequence. They already know you. A cold opener to someone who booked a call reads like you forgot them.

## Count the right things

- A call counts on the day the person **books**, not the day of the call. Calls booked this week for next Tuesday are
  this week's work.
- A rebook is not a new person. A reschedule is not a new call. Count people.
- Rates divide by **new people contacted**, never by messages sent.
