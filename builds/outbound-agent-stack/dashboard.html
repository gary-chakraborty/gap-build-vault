The rule that makes it trustworthyA blocked source has to fail loudly. Returning an empty list when a site blocks you is banned. One run had Reddit quietly blocked, the empty result looked like a finished report, and the whole buyer profile for accountants got built out of a subreddit for financial advisers. It shipped into live posts before anyone noticed. Empty must never look like blocked.
02

The Buyer Psychologist

Finds the words the buyer uses themselves — what actually worries them at six in the evening, not what their job title says. Reads real threads and real reviews, and quotes them. Never invents a phrase.

skill: icp-psychographic-profiler Reddit forums review bodies comment threads
  • This is the agent that made the money on this campaign. The subject line that got the reply was "who reconciles the trust account." Nobody at our end wrote that phrase. It is what small law firms call it. That is the only reason the email did not read like a bookkeeping ad.
  • The fear underneath it: get the client trust ledger wrong and you can lose your licence. Not your money — your licence. No amount of clever writing gets you to that sentence. You have to go and read what they said.
  • Runs before any offer, email or deck exists. A shallow profile makes everything downstream shallow.
Agents 3, 4 & 5The list is the campaign

03

The Offer Writer

Turns all that research into one promise, for one kind of company, with one number in it. Then two or three different ways of saying that same promise.

skill: b2b-client-acquisitionno API needed
  • The test it has to pass: could two people fill this in two different ways and both be right? If yes, it is not nailed down, and it will get invented differently every time.
  • Written once, properly, by the best model you have. Then a cheap model fills it in hundreds of times. That split is the whole cost model — the judgment lives in the rules, not in the model.
04

The List Builder

Finds companies that match, and throws out everything that only looks like it matches. Picks the cheapest source that can do the job, automatically, and never asks which tool to use.

skill: lead-list-buildersource waterfallfree engine first
  • Cheapest first, every time: a export you already paid for → the free scraper → a tool that came bundled with something else → prepaid credits → new money, last.
  • A source that has run out of credit is skipped, not fatal. Only running out of every source stops the build. This rule exists because one provider failed mid-build and the suggested fix was a new subscription — while thousands of already-paid credits sat unused somewhere else.

05

The Contact Finder

Finds the right person at each firm and an address that actually works. Uses a chain of sources, never a single one.

contact chainemail chainemail verifier
  • Who they are: the company's own site → a search API → a person-search tool → a data provider.
  • Their email: a match endpoint that returns a checked address → an enrichment tool → guessing the pattern, last and least.
  • Every address then goes through a verifier. If it cannot be verified it is dropped, never guessed. A guessed address is a bounce, and bounces cost you the whole inbox.
Why it is a chain and not one toolOne build went single-source and lost 744 firms that the other tools in the chain would have covered. One provider is a single point of failure wearing a budget line.
Agent 6The biggest lever, and the cheapest

Then you work out who is worth emailing this week.

06

The Signal Finder

A signal is something that changed at their company. They hired someone. They moved. They posted a job. They raised money. Your list tells you whether you could ever email them. The signal tells you whether to email them now.

signal doctrinejob boardsdig TXTfree page scrape
  • No signal, no send. If you cannot name the thing that changed and put it in the first line of the email, that lead goes on a watch list, not into a campaign.
  • Filter on fit BEFORE you spend a credit, using the free data the platform already gives you — title, headcount, industry, country. Enriching first burns money on people you were never going to email.
The signalBest used insideDead after
A public "anyone know a tool for this?" post24 hoursa few days
A job ad for the role our thing replaces3–5 days30 days
A funding round closing48 hours from spotting it60 days

Free places to find signals that most people already have access to and never use: job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby), a check for a dead blog, a DNS lookup that shows which email tool they run, and a page scrape that shows whether they have booking or chat installed.

A date filter is a hint, not proofOn one "they are hiring" list, 41% had no live job ad at all — and one supposedly fresh posting turned out to be ten months old. The platform's own date filter said otherwise. Go back to the record that carries the signal and check it yourself before you put it in line one.

Agents 7 & 8Only now does anyone write anything

Then two emails go out. That is all. Ever.

07

The Email Writer

Fills in a script that already works. It does not write a new one. It names the template and the line it is using before it writes a word.

skill: email-outreachscript librarycheap model fills it
  • First email is under 64 words. The one below is 40.
  • Cold outreach is two emails. The first, then one follow-up, then stop. No third. No fourth. No "just bumping this."
  • Plain text. No bullets, no bold, no formatting in the body. It has to read like a person typed it.
  • Line one is the signal. Line two is the result that signal implies — never a pain line, never the offer.
  • No price, no case study, no proof numbers in the first email. Those belong in the follow-up.
who reconciles the trust account Email 1
Hi {first name}, Saw {their firm} handles {their practice area} work out of {their city}. We run the books and the monthly trust reconciliation for small law firms. Nobody there has to sit and check the ledger. Can I send some more info?
↳ same thread, no new subject Follow-up, day 2
Hey {first name}, Just got some time to get back to you. Pulled together a month-end trust account checklist — the red flags worth catching yourself before an auditor does. Want me to send it over?

Four moves in the first email, in this order: a true fact about them, what we do in their own words, taking a person off a job they hate, and asking permission instead of asking for a meeting. The follow-up is the one that got the reply — it does not ask for anything, it offers something the reader would keep.

08

The Inbox Agent

Reads every reply the minute it lands, sorts it, and writes the exact message to send back. A person presses send. Nobody sits and drafts.

skill: inbox-managementwebhook from the sendernotification carries the draft
  • Sorts every reply green, yellow or red and sends it down the matching path.
  • A positive reply and an objection are different scripts, never the same one.
  • Books with two specific times. Never a calendar link.
  • Never quotes a price. Not a figure, not a range, not even when pushed.
  • Answering inside five minutes tells a prospect they are dealing with a real operation. Every minute after five is leverage you gave away.

The point is not speed. It is being able to rebuild the same afternoon.

Six campaigns, 1,576 checked leads, forty sending inboxes each — built in 2 minutes and 1 second. That used to be a full day of work.

Speed is not the advantage. Here is the advantage: when a list is wrong, or the angle does not land, you find out in three days and you rebuild it that afternoon instead of next quarter. Most outbound dies because the feedback loop is a month long and by the time you know, nobody remembers what you changed.

Run the chain once and you can run it again on a different niche before lunch. That is what makes a bad result cheap instead of fatal.

Setting it upThe short version

You need five things, and the order matters more than the tools.

Get the files

Everything is at github.com/gary-chakraborty/gap-build-vault, in the builds/outbound-agent-stack folder. Clone it, or just read it in the browser. There is no email wall and no sign-up.

The five things you need

A free scraper, a search API key, one contact provider you already pay for, an email verifier, and a sending tool. That is the entire stack. If you already run outbound, you probably have four of the five.

Run the agents in order

One to eight. Do not skip to the email. The research step is what produced the subject line that got the reply, and you cannot reverse-engineer it afterwards.

Your first run should be small

One kind of company. One signal. Two hundred leads. Not five thousand. You are finding out whether the signal exists, not chasing volume — and a bad list at five thousand costs you your sending domain.

Then measure by signal, never blended

Track reply rate for each signal type separately. A blended number hides the one lane that is working and the three that are not.

The full walkthrough — keys, permissions, and every setting — is its own thing. It is coming as a separate video, because doing it properly takes longer than a section.
