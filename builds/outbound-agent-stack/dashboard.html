Setting it up · the short version

Five things. The order matters more than the tools.

1
Get the files

All of it is at github.com/gary-chakraborty/gap-build-vault, folder builds/outbound-agent-stack. No sign-up.

2
What you need

A free scraper, a search key, one contact tool you already pay for, an email verifier, a sending tool. If you run outbound you probably have four of the five.

3
Run them in order

One to eight. Do not skip to the email. The research is what produced the subject line, and you cannot work backwards to it.

4
Start small

One kind of company. One signal. Two hundred leads, not five thousand. A bad list at five thousand costs you your sending domain.

5
Measure by signal, not blended

A blended reply rate hides the one lane that works and the three that do not.

The full install — keys, permissions, every setting — is its own video. Doing it properly takes longer than a section.
