# Before you send anything

Eight questions. A blank one is where your campaign leaks.

Answer them out loud. If you cannot answer one in a sentence, you have found the leak —
go back to the agent that owns it before you send.

---

**1. Can you name the one kind of company you are writing to, in three words?**
Owned by agent 3. "B2B companies" is not an answer. "Small California law firms" is.

**2. Can you name what they are afraid of, in their words?**
Owned by agent 2. If the phrase came out of your own head, it is not their words.

**3. Did somebody actually open the websites on your list and read them?**
Owned by agent 4. Not a keyword filter. Opened, and read. On one real list, 27% turned
out not to be the kind of company they appeared to be.

**4. Is every email address verified, or are some of them guesses?**
Owned by agent 5. A guessed pattern is fine if it is labelled and separated. Mixed into
the main send, it costs you the domain.

**5. For each lead, can you name what changed at their company?**
Owned by agent 6. Not a fact about them. A change, with a date.

**6. Is that still fresh — checked at the source, not at a filter?**
Owned by agent 6. On one list, 41% of "they are hiring" leads had no live ad at all.

**7. Is your first email under 64 words, with no price in it?**
Owned by agent 7. Count the words. Over means cut.

**8. Does someone answer a reply inside five minutes?**
Owned by agent 8. If the honest answer is "when I next open the inbox", that is the
cheapest fix on this whole page.

---

## If you only fix one thing

Most people fix these backwards.

| Order | What | Why it is first | The number |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The signal** | Can you name what changed at their company? If not, you are guessing, and they can tell | only **15%** of leads survive this gate |
| 2 | **The list** | Are these really the businesses you think they are? Usually a quarter are not | **27%** were the wrong kind of company |
| 3 | **The words** | Last. A great email to the wrong list still gets you nothing | generic openers stall around **0.4%** |

Everyone rewrites the email first. It is the cheapest thing to change and the least
likely to be the problem.

*Numbers measured 2026-07-22 and 2026-08-20 on US bookkeeping and accounting firms,
3 to 50 staff. Yours will differ. Measure your own.*
