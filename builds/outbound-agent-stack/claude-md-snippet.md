# Your business block

Every one of the eight agents reads this. Fill it in once, paste it into your Claude
project as knowledge, and never type any of it again.

Fill in **every** bracket. If you do not have the thing, write what you do have. Never
leave a bracket sitting there. An empty slot is where an agent invents something, and
an invented price or an invented client result reaches a real buyer.

```
MY BUSINESS

Company name: [your company]
What I sell, in one sentence a buyer would say back to me: [not "we help businesses
grow" — the sentence you would say out loud on a call]
What it costs: [real number, or a real range, or "we never quote before a call"]
How I charge: [one off, monthly retainer, per project, revenue share]

WHO I SELL TO

The kind of company: [industry, in the words they use for themselves]
How big: [headcount range and revenue range]
Where: [countries, states, cities]
The exact job titles who sign: [3 to 5 titles, not departments]
Who is NOT a buyer, even though they look like one: [the lookalikes that waste your
time — write them down, agent 4 uses this to bin them]

MY PROOF

Result 1: [client type, what changed, the real number, how long it took]
Result 2: [same shape]
Result 3: [same shape, or write "only two so far"]
What I am allowed to say publicly: [named clients, anonymous only, or numbers only.
This is a permission list. If a client has not said yes in writing, they are anonymous]

MY GUARANTEE

[The exact words. If you have none, write "no guarantee yet" so nothing gets invented]

HOW I SOUND

Reading level: 7th grade. Short sentences. No corporate words.
Words I never use: [your banned list — "solutions", "leverage", "synergy", whatever
makes you cringe]
Words my buyers actually use for their own problem: [leave this blank for now.
Agent 2 fills it in, and it is the most valuable line on this page]

MY RULES

Never put a price in a first email.
Never send a calendar link. Offer two real times instead.
Cold outreach is two messages. First one, then one follow-up. Then stop.
First email is under 64 words.
Every number I state carries the day it was measured.
```

## After agent 2 runs

Come back and fill in the "words my buyers actually use" line with the phrases agent 2
lifted word for word. Then every agent after it writes in the buyer's language instead
of yours.

That one edit is the difference between an email that sounds like you and an email that
sounds like them.
